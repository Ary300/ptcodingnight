Park Tudor Coding Night

Coding Night is the Park Tudor Computer Science Club’s annual programming contest. Each student is given their own problems, and after the first 30 minutes teammates can work together to solve them.

Built by Park Tudor students

Coding Night previously ran on HackerRank, with team scores calculated by hand afterward. This year, members of the Computer Science Club built this site to judge submissions and calculate team scores automatically.

How Coding Night works

  1. 1

    Enter your join code

    Each student receives a slip with a join code at check-in. Entering the code signs you in and places you on your team.

  2. 2

    Find your team

    Your slip lists your team and your problem set.

  3. 3

    Solve your problems

    Each student receives one easy and two medium problems. For the first 30 minutes everyone works alone, and after that teammates can help each other.

  4. 4

    Follow the standings

    Submissions are graded within seconds, and the standings on the projector update live until the last fifteen minutes, when the board freezes.

Format and schedule

Pizza is served at 6:30. The contest runs from 7:00 to 8:15, followed by a Kahoot and awards, and the evening ends by 8:45.

  • Teams

    Teams have up to four students, and each teammate receives different problems. A team’s score is the average of its members’ scores.

  • Puzzles

    During the contest, each team member also receives one puzzle that does not require programming, such as a sudoku. Solving it adds points to the team’s score.

  • Kahoot

    A short Kahoot follows the contest, and the results count toward the final standings.

  • Frozen standings

    The standings are hidden for the final 15 minutes and revealed at the awards.
